Privacy Policy — Personal Uploader

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Personal Uploader ("the App") is a private tool that publishes videos to its owner's TikTok account using the official TikTok API.

Data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information (scope user.info.basic) needed to identify the connected account.
  • Permission to publish videos (scope video.publish).
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok.

How data is used and stored

Tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer and are used solely to publish videos to the connected account. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings under "Security > Manage app permissions"; the local tokens are then deleted.
